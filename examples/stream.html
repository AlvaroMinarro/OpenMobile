WebCodecs H.264 decode of the bridge /v1/stream/video WebSocket.

starting…

Tap the canvas to inject a tap through the control socket. Query params: ?url= overrides the video WS URL (default ws://127.0.0.1:8765/v1/stream/video). Requires a Chromium-based browser (WebCodecs+AVC); Firefox shows the polling-fallback status. The bridge must run with streaming enabled and NO OPENMOBILE_BRIDGE_SECRET — browsers cannot attach the X-OpenMobile-Secret header to WebSocket upgrades.
